SageFin Desktop

Open a retailer, sign in as you normally would, and look at your orders. Each orders page you open is saved on this computer.

This computer cannot protect saved data, so nothing will be saved.

    SageFin Desktop only reads the orders pages you open yourself. It does not browse, click or sign in for you. Saved pages are sent to SageFin once this computer is connected, in Settings, under Retail sync.
